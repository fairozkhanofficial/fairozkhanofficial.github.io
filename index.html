Multi-Modal Evidence Review Pipeline

Rank #79 / 1,773

HackerRank Orchestrate, June 2026. Can a two-call pipeline adjudicate damage claims from photos alone?

Built a production-style system that processes insurance-style damage claims (car, laptop, package) by treating submitted images as the primary evidence. One multimodal vision call extracts per-image findings, one text-only call reasons over them. Everything else is deterministic: sufficiency gating, risk flags, schema enforcement. Ran a controlled four-config experiment, shipped the winner, and added multi-key failover with round-robin rotation for reliability under quota limits.
